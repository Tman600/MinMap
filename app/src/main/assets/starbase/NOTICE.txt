Changes made for MinMap: rocket-starship.glb was split into booster.glb and ship.glb, and
starbase-tower.glb into tower.glb, carriage.glb (the frame the chopsticks ride on),
arm-left.glb and arm-right.glb (the chopstick arms) and qd-arm.glb (the ship quick-disconnect
arm), each moved so its origin is the point it turns about, so the parts can be animated during
a launch. starbase-mount.glb is unchanged (saved as mount.glb). The geometry and materials are
otherwise as published.

flame.glb, vapor.glb and steam.glb (exhaust, venting and deluge steam) are MinMap's own, generated
for it, and are not part of munim-maps-vehicles.
